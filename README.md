# voiceprint

Train Claude on **your** personal writing voice, then have it write anything in your tone.

It starts with an intensive interview (and your real writing samples), builds a saved voice profile, and from then on writes DMs, emails, posts and lead outreach that read as if you wrote them. Clean spelling, human rhythm, no AI tells.

Made for creators, operators and anyone who sends a lot of messages and wants them to still sound human.

---

## Install (Claude Code)

```
/plugin marketplace add __GHUSER__/voiceprint
/plugin install voiceprint@voiceprint
```

Then reload:

```
/reload-plugins
```

## How to use

Run the skill: `/voiceprint:voiceprint`

**1. Train it (once):**
Say "interview mich" or "lern meine Sprache". It runs a deep interview, asks for 3 to 5 real messages you actually sent, mirrors your style back, and saves your voice profile.

**2. Write in your voice:**
"Schreib mir eine DM für Lead X in meinem Ton." It writes it as you would.

**3. Humanize:**
Paste any text or AI draft: "zieh das durch meinen Ton." It rewrites it in your voice.

## What it does NOT do

It does not send messages for you and it does not automate outreach. It writes, you send. That protects your accounts and keeps everything in your hands.

## The core rules

- Your tone, your quirks — but spelling and grammar always fixed.
- Simple punctuation (comma, period, ?, !). No em-dashes, no decorative colons.
- No AI filler. Human rhythm. Never invents facts.
